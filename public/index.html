        About

        A small, free corner of the night

        Lofi Skylines is a free, open-source web app: animated pixel-art cities with 30 generated tracks across lo-fi, deep focus and focused EDM. Every track is composed live in your browser. No account, no downloads, no ads.

        Every scene is drawn on a 480 × 270 canvas, one pixel at a time. Every note of the music — and every drop of rain, gust of wind and clear-night cricket — is synthesized with the Web Audio API. There are 30 tracks across lo-fi, deep focus and focused EDM; the queue moves on by itself when a track ends, or press Next to skip ahead. Nothing is streamed, sampled, or pre-recorded.

        Built as a minimal tribute to Lofi Cities. The code is MIT licensed. Add your own city by editing js/cities.js and js/scenes.js.
